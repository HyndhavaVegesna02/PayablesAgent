---
id: CHG-057
title: The owner links a credit to an invoice (explain_credit), mirroring the debit side (PO, batch 21)
type: feature
---

No owner action linked a credit to a receivable: the reconciler's unknown_txn and ambiguous_match cases for a
credit only fed the owner's answer back to the model, so the credit stayed UNMATCHED, the receivable stayed open,
and the planner counted the money twice. A receivable also closed only on one credit of exactly its amount.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] AC1: A credit the reconciler can't match raises one explain_credit question, built by code, linked to its
  case: every open receivable offered for unknown_txn, the named ones for ambiguous_match.
- [ ] AC2: The owner links the credit to a receivable: the credit MATCHED and the receivable CONFIRMED, both as the
  owner, through the writer. A different amount is stated in the event, exactly ("short by ₹X" or "over by ₹X");
  the receivable's amount_paise is never edited.
- [ ] AC3: "Not an invoice" leaves the credit UNMATCHED and settles the case. A stale page, a credit settled
  meanwhile, and a helper are refused or closed as on the debit side.
- [ ] AC4: Needs attention shows the credit, the receivables to choose from (none pre-selected), and, when the
  case has one, the agent's finding (its summary and the messages it cited) as the assistant's words (PO).
- [ ] AC5: Answering closes the case's agent question too; the plan is redone and no longer counts the money twice.
